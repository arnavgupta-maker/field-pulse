Institutional context and the employment effects of artificial intelligence in European SMEs

While your foundational reading focuses primarily on US task exposures and firm-level productivity, this paper expands the automation literature by showing how national institutional frameworks moderate AI’s impact on European SMEs. Crucially, it demonstrates that while AI adoption generally correlates with firm-level job growth, strict Employment Protection Legislation (EPL) and political centralization significantly attenuate these positive employment gains.
