AI exposure, occupational mobility, and post-transition job quality in China: labor-saving and labor-augmenting channels

This paper advances the task-based framework (ALM) and recent AI exposure literature (such as *GPTs are GPTs*) by empirically disentangling labor-saving from labor-augmenting AI channels using a causal IV setup in an emerging market (China). It directly extends your bibliography's focus on productivity and displacement by demonstrating how these two distinct AI channels differentially dictate workers' occupational switching distances, transition barriers for low-skilled labor, and post-move job quality.
