Can the fourth industrial revolution solve the productivity problem?

This paper directly pushes back on the productivity optimism found in *Generative AI at Work* and *GPTs are GPTs* by shifting the focus from firm-level task efficiency to the macro-institutional mechanisms that dictate capital versus labor income shares. It extends the task-displacement concerns of Autor et al. and Acemoglu & Restrepo, arguing that GenAI’s impact on specialized knowledge workers will likely exacerbate wage-productivity decoupling unless actively steered by state policy.
