AI IN HEALTHCARE - THE MEDICAL STATUS IN TRANSFORMATION: CAN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ALTER THE FOUNDATIONS OF PROFESSIONAL POWER?

This paper extends the task-based framework (Autor et al.) and high-skill LLM exposure models (*GPTs are GPTs*) to elite cognitive professions by examining how AI disrupts the institutional monopolies and occupational rents long enjoyed by medical professionals.
