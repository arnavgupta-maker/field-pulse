THE ROLE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN TRANSFORMING INDIA'S ECONOMY: IMPLICATIONS FOR EMPLOYMENT, PRODUCTIVITY, AND ECONOMIC GROWTH

This paper extends the structural labor market frameworks of Acemoglu, Autor, and Brynjolfsson beyond advanced economies to a major emerging market, analyzing how generative AI and automation interact with informal labor, uneven digital infrastructure, and large working-age populations. By examining sectoral transformations in Indian IT, BPM, and agriculture through 2026, it provides critical empirical insight into how AI adoption simultaneously drives productivity and threatens mid-skill displacement in developing economies.
