AI & Cognitive Work

While the empirical papers in your library (Autor et al., Brynjolfsson et al.) treat cognitive work as high-skill, premium-earning tasks that are either resilient to automation or boosted by AI, this paper argues that GenAI will structurally collapse the historical link between cognitive labor, credentials, and wage premiums. Furthermore, it expands the exposure models of Eloundou et al. by introducing a long-term human capital externality: ubiquitous cognitive assistance threatens the development of executive function required to produce skilled workers in the first place.
