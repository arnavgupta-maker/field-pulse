Robotics vs. Generative AI: Why They Exhibit Differing Labour Market Effects in Australia

This paper directly synthesizes the distinct streams in your reading list by using Autor, Levy, and Murnane's task-based framework to explicitly contrast the economic transmission channels of industrial robotics (Acemoglu & Restrepo) against generative AI (Eloundou et al.; Brynjolfsson et al.). By leveraging Australian sectoral data, it demonstrates that physical robotics causes concentrated, localized displacement shocks while GenAI drives geographically diffuse task augmentation, providing empirical backing for why "automation" requires distinct, technology-specific policy responses rather than a monolithic framework.
