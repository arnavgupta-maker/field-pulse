Does Technological Advancement Widen Income Inequality? Evidence From the Impact of Generative Artificial Intelligence on the Internal Pay Gap Within Enterprises

While existing work like Brynjolfsson et al. shows GenAI compressing skill disparities among frontline workers, this paper extends task-based inequality frameworks (ALM; Acemoglu & Restrepo) by showing that GenAI widens the vertical intra-firm pay gap between managers and ordinary employees. By linking LLM exposure metrics to firm-level compensation data, it provides empirical evidence that GenAI acts primarily as a cognitive complement to management, converting executive productivity gains into wider wage dispersion when rank-and-file workers lack bargaining power.
