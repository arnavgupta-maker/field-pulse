FACTORS INFLUENCING THE SPATIAL LABOR EMPLOYMENT STRUCTURE IN THE DIGITAL ECONOMY: BASED ON A SPATIAL PANEL MODEL

This paper complements Acemoglu and Restrepo’s regional approach by evaluating spatial spillovers in China, shifting the focus from specific automation technologies (like robots or LLMs) to broader digital economy infrastructure. Crucially for your focus on task and skill dynamics, it offers a counterpoint to standard skill-biased technological change by showing that regional digital growth disproportionately expands employment capacity for low- and medium-skilled workers through local agglomeration effects.
