Scaling up to the cloud: Cloud technology use and growth rates in small and large firms

This paper directly extends the task-based framework of Autor, Levy, and Murnane (2003) to modern cloud infrastructure, demonstrating how SaaS adoption reorganizes firm labor away from routine clerical and manual occupations toward managerial and ICT roles. Crucially, while technologies like industrial robots (Acemoglu & Restrepo) disproportionately favor large incumbents and increase concentration, this paper reveals that cloud technology acts as a scale-equalizing tool that boosts small-firm growth, provided firms possess the complementary ICT capabilities highlighted in the Generative AI literature.
